Gravitor Privacy Policy

Cnmbuck Studios · Last updated 17 November 2026

The short version

Gravitor has no accounts and does not collect your personal information. Your progress and settings stay on your iPhone. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects limited device and usage data to deliver and measure those ads. The ads are non-personalised, and Gravitor does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Data stored on your device

Level progress, stars, best times and settings (such as tilt sensitivity) are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Motion sensor data is used live, to turn your phone's tilt into ball movement. It is not recorded or sent anywhere.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Gravitor uses Google AdMob to show an occasional ad between levels, and an optional ad you can choose to watch to skip a level. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers and information (for example device model and OS version)
  • approximate location derived from your IP address
  • ad interactions (ads shown and tapped)
  • diagnostic and performance data

Gravitor requests non-personalised ads only and never asks for permission to track you. How Google uses this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you buy Remove Ads, Gravitor stops loading ads entirely.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. The app only checks with the App Store whether you own Remove Ads.

Children

Gravitor is rated 4+. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Ads are non-personalised.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. For questions, see the Gravitor support page.
