Common questions

The ball drifts or doesn't respond to tilt

Hold the phone how you like to play, then tap ◎ on the game screen (or Settings → Calibrate now) to make that position "level". You can also adjust Tilt sensitivity, Dead zone and Smoothing in Settings, or use Reset tilt to defaults.

I'd rather not tilt

Go to Settings → Controls → Touch to steer with an on-screen joystick.

I'm stuck on a level

After three falls on a level you can choose to skip it. Free players watch a short ad to skip; with Remove Ads the skip is free.

I bought Remove Ads on another device, or after reinstalling

Go to Settings → Restore purchases.

How do I get the Cnmbuck Orb?

Finish 50 levels, or buy Remove Ads, which also unlocks it.
